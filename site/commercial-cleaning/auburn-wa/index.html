FAQ

Questions Facility Managers Ask Us

Does Rainier have an office in Auburn?

No. Rainier Facility Solutions is based at 823 Main Street, Suite 2, in Sumner, and we dispatch crews to Auburn from there. Your free walkthrough, onboarding and every recurring visit happen on site at your Auburn building, and your account manager is reachable directly whether you're near Lea Hill, the SuperMall corridor or downtown.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Auburn?

There's no flat rate we can quote over the phone. After a free walkthrough of your Auburn facility, we put together a written, room-by-room scope of work and set a flat monthly price based on the space, frequency and services you need. Most quotes are back within one business day.

Can you clean our Auburn facility after hours or on weekends?

Yes. After-hours, overnight and weekend cleaning is standard for our Auburn accounts, not an upcharge. Many of our clients in the industrial valley and Lea Hill run shift schedules, so we build the cleaning window around your operating hours rather than the other way around.

What happens if your crew misses something?

Report it in writing within 24 hours of the visit and we'll send the crew back to re-clean those areas at no charge. This is a re-clean guarantee, not a refund, and it applies to every recurring account we service in Auburn and across our other service areas.

Do you clean medical and dental offices in Auburn?

Yes. Auburn has a number of medical and dental practices, and our crews working those accounts are trained in OSHA-aware sanitation practices and HIPAA privacy rules. Bloodborne-pathogen-trained staff are available where a practice requires it, and we use color-coded equipment to avoid cross-contamination between areas.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes for our building?

Keys, alarm codes, badges and access instructions are documented during onboarding, before your first cleaning visit. Only your assigned crew uses them. If a key is ever lost or compromised, we replace it at our expense, which covers rekeying costs where needed.

Are we locked into a long-term contract?

No. Recurring service runs on a simple service agreement that either side can end with 30 days' notice. One-time cleaning jobs, like a post-construction cleanup or a deep clean before an inspection, don't require a contract at all.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage and can provide a certificate of insurance naming your business as additional insured on request. Every crew member is also bonded, background-checked and drug-screened before being assigned to a site.

Do you handle post-construction cleanup for new builds or tenant improvements?

Yes, post-construction cleanup is one of the services most requested by Auburn clients, alongside janitorial and floor care. We work rough, final and touch-up phases, clearing drywall dust, paint splatter and adhesive residue while coordinating around your superintendent's punch-list schedule.

What if we have a flood, sewage backup or other emergency?

Call 253-447-5676 and it goes to our on-call dispatch, 24 hours a day. We mobilize as fast as logistics allow for water and flood cleanup, sewage backups and biohazard situations, and coordinate with restoration partners for any dry-out work beyond our scope.

Are your cleaning products eco-friendly?

Eco-friendly products, guided by standards like EPA Safer Choice, are our default across Auburn accounts, chosen to be safer for staff, tenants and visitors. For jobs that call for something stronger, like grout restoration or post-construction cleanup, we bring the right product for the task.

We have more than one building. Can you put them on one schedule?

Yes. If you manage multiple properties, whether in Auburn or across Pierce, King and Snohomish counties, we can set up one schedule, one point of contact and one invoice cycle. Each building still gets its own free walkthrough and written scope of work.

How soon can you start service at our Auburn facility?

Most walkthroughs are scheduled within a few business days of your call, with a written scope and flat monthly price following within one business day after that. Once the scope is signed, recurring service usually begins one to two weeks later.
