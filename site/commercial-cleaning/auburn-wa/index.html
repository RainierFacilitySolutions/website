FAQ

Questions Facility Managers Ask Us

Do you have an office in Auburn?

No. Rainier Facility Solutions is based at 823 Main Street, Suite 2 in Sumner, and our crews serve Auburn, including Lea Hill, the SuperMall corridor, downtown and the industrial valley, from that headquarters. Your free walkthrough and all cleaning still happen on site at your building.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Auburn?

We don't publish flat rates because every building is different. After a free walkthrough of your Auburn facility, we write up a room-by-room scope of work and set a flat monthly price based on that scope, so there are no hidden line items later.

Can you clean after hours or on weekends in Auburn?

Yes. Before-opening, after-close, overnight and weekend cleaning is standard for our Auburn accounts, not a premium add-on. Many of the offices and industrial buildings we clean in the valley run on shift schedules, and we build visits around those hours.

What if your crew misses something?

Report it in writing within 24 hours of the visit and we'll return to re-clean the affected areas at no charge. That's our 100% satisfaction guarantee, and it applies to every recurring account in Auburn.

Do you clean medical and dental offices in Auburn?

Yes, medical and dental practices are among the building types we clean in Auburn. Our staff is HIPAA-trained, and we have bloodborne-pathogen-trained crews available along with color-coded equipment for sanitation-sensitive areas.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes for our building?

We document keys, alarm codes, badge access and supply preferences during onboarding, before your first visit. Access is used only by your assigned crew, and if a key is lost or compromised, we replace it at our expense.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No. Recurring service runs on a simple service agreement that either side can end with 30 days' notice. One-time cleaning jobs don't involve a contract at all.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage and can provide a certificate of insurance naming your business as additional insured on request. Every team member is also bonded, insured and background-checked.

Do you handle post-construction cleanup for new builds in Auburn?

Yes, post-construction cleaning is one of the most requested services we provide in Auburn's industrial valley and surrounding business areas. We work rough, final and touch-up phases, clearing drywall dust, paint splatter and debris while coordinating around the project's punch-list trades.

How soon can service start after the walkthrough?

Most walkthroughs are scheduled within a few business days, and you'll have a written scope and flat monthly price within one business day after that. Once the scope is signed, recurring service for your Auburn facility usually begins one to two weeks later.
