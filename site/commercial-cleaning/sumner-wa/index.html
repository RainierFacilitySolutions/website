FAQ

Questions Facility Managers Ask Us

Does Rainier Facility Solutions have an office in Sumner?

Yes. Our headquarters is at 823 Main Street, Suite 2, in downtown Sumner. It's where our dispatch, account management and crew scheduling are based, and it's the starting point for every walkthrough and crew assignment across the 49 cities we serve.

What does commercial cleaning cost in Sumner?

There's no flat rate we can quote without seeing your space. We start every recurring account with a free walkthrough, then write a room-by-room scope of work and set a flat monthly price based on that scope. That way you know exactly what's included and what you're paying each month.

Can you clean our building after hours or on weekends?

Yes. Most of our recurring cleaning in Sumner happens before opening, after close, overnight or on weekends, whichever keeps the work out of your team's way. We schedule around warehouse shift changes, retail hours and office closing times as needed.

What if your crew misses something during a cleaning?

Report it to us in writing within 24 hours of the visit and we'll send the crew back to re-clean those specific areas at no charge. That's our 100% satisfaction guarantee, and it applies to every recurring account.

Do you clean medical and dental offices in Sumner?

Yes. We clean medical and dental practices with OSHA-aware sanitation practices, color-coded equipment and HIPAA-trained staff. Bloodborne-pathogen-trained crews are available for facilities that need that level of care.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes for our Sumner facility?

We document keys, alarm codes, badge access and supply preferences during onboarding, before your first cleaning visit. Only your assigned crew uses that access. If a key is ever lost or compromised, we replace it at our expense.

Are we locked into a long-term contract?

No. Recurring service runs on a simple service agreement that either side can end with 30 days' notice. One-time cleaning jobs don't require a contract at all.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage, and every team member is bonded. We'll provide a certificate of insurance naming your business as additional insured on request.

Do you clean up after construction projects in Sumner?

Yes. We handle post-construction cleanup for new builds, tenant improvements and renovations in the valley and the Sumner-Pacific industrial corridor, working in rough, final and touch-up phases and coordinating with your superintendent or project manager.

What happens if we have a flood or sewage emergency?

Call 253-447-5676. We're open 24/7 with on-call dispatch, and we mobilize as fast as logistics allow for water damage, sewage backups and biohazard situations. Our crews handle the cleanup and coordinate dry-out with restoration partners as needed.

Are your cleaning products eco-friendly?

Eco-friendly products are our default, guided by EPA Safer Choice and similar standards, so they're safer for your staff, tenants and visitors. For jobs that call for something stronger, like grout or post-construction cleanup, we use the right product for the task.

How soon can service start for our Sumner building?

Most walkthroughs are scheduled within a few business days, and you'll have a written scope and price within one business day after that. Recurring service usually begins one to two weeks after the scope is signed, giving us time to handle onboarding and key access properly.
