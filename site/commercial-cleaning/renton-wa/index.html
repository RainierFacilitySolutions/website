FAQ

Questions Facility Managers Ask Us

Does Rainier Facility Solutions have an office in Renton?

No. Our office is at 823 Main Street, Suite 2 in Sumner. Crews are dispatched from there to clean facilities throughout Renton, including offices near The Landing, downtown Renton and the Highlands. Your free walkthrough happens on site at your building, not at our office.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Renton?

There is no set price list. We start with a free walkthrough of your facility, then put together a written, room-by-room scope of work and set a flat monthly price based on what that scope covers. There are no hidden line items added later.

Can you clean our Renton office after hours or on weekends?

Yes. Most recurring cleaning for Renton facilities happens before opening, after close, overnight or on weekends so it does not interrupt your staff or customers. This is standard scheduling for us, not a premium add-on, and it works well for buildings near the airport that run on shift schedules.

What happens if your crew misses something?

Report it to us in writing within 24 hours of the visit and we will return to re-clean the affected areas at no charge. This is a re-clean guarantee, not a refund. It applies to every recurring account across Renton and our other service areas.

Do you clean medical and dental offices in Renton?

Yes. We clean medical and dental practices in Renton with OSHA-aware sanitation practices, color-coded equipment and HIPAA-trained staff. Bloodborne-pathogen-trained crews are available for facilities that need them. Every team member is background-checked and drug-screened before being assigned to a site.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes for our Renton building?

Keys, alarm codes, badges and supply preferences are documented during onboarding, before the first cleaning visit. Only your assigned crew uses this access. If a key is ever lost or compromised, we replace it at our expense.

Do we have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Recurring service runs on a simple service agreement that either side can end with 30 days' notice. One-time cleaning jobs, like a deep clean or post-construction cleanup, have no contract at all.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage and can provide a certificate of insurance naming your business as additional insured on request. Our team members are W-2 employees, not subcontractors, and each is bonded and insured.

Do you handle post-construction cleanup for new builds or remodels near the airport corridor?

Yes. We clean up drywall dust, paint splatter, adhesive residue and debris in rough, final and touch-up phases, working around the schedules of other trades on site. This is a frequently requested service for commercial properties in and around Renton.

What if we have a flood or sewage emergency at our Renton facility?

Call 253-447-5676. Our on-call dispatch handles emergency calls 24 hours a day, and we mobilize as fast as logistics allow for water damage, sewage backups and biohazard situations. We coordinate with restoration partners on dry-out as needed.
