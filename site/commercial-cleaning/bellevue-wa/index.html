FAQ

Questions Facility Managers Ask Us

Does Rainier Facility Solutions have an office in Bellevue?

No. Rainier Facility Solutions is headquartered at 823 Main Street, Suite 2 in Sumner, and we serve Bellevue from there. Your free walkthrough happens on site at your Bellevue building, and a crew is scheduled to your location from our Sumner headquarters.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Bellevue?

There's no published rate because every building is different. We start with a free walkthrough of your Bellevue facility, then send a written room-by-room scope of work and a flat monthly price within one business day. You'll know exactly what's included before you sign anything.

Can you clean our office tower after hours or on weekends?

Yes. After-hours, overnight and weekend cleaning is standard, not an upgrade. Many Bellevue office towers and medical buildings need work scheduled around badge access and tenant rules, and our recurring visits are set up around whatever schedule works for your building.

What if your crew misses something?

Report it in writing within 24 hours of the visit and we'll return to re-clean the affected areas at no charge. It's a re-clean, not a refund, and it applies to every recurring account we service, including those in Bellevue.

Do you clean medical and dental offices?

Yes. We clean medical and dental practices in Bellevue and across King County. Our staff completes OSHA and HIPAA training, and bloodborne-pathogen-trained crews are available for clinical spaces. We use color-coded equipment and eco-friendly products by default to help limit cross-contamination.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes for our building?

Keys, codes, badges and supply preferences are documented during onboarding, before your first cleaning visit. Only your assigned crew uses them. If a key is ever lost or compromised, we replace it at our expense.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Recurring service runs on a simple service agreement that either side can end with 30 days' notice. One-time cleaning jobs, including deep cleans or post-construction cleanup in Bellevue, have no contract at all.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage, and we can provide a certificate of insurance naming your business as additional insured on request. Every team member sent to a Bellevue site is bonded, insured and background-checked.

Do you handle post-construction cleanup for tenant improvements?

Yes. We clean new builds, tenant improvements and renovations in rough, final and touch-up phases, including drywall dust, paint splatter, adhesive residue, fixtures and floors. With Bellevue's ongoing downtown construction, this is a common request from builders and property owners.

What do I do if we have a flood or sewage backup after hours?

Call 253-447-5676. We're open 24/7 with on-call dispatch for water damage, sewage backups and biohazard situations, and we mobilize as fast as logistics allow. We handle the cleanup and coordinate dry-out with restoration partners as needed.
